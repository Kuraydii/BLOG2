'Fantastic Four: First Steps' Preview: Retro-Futurism Meets Multiversal Incursions

Published: 4 Days Ago • Reading Time: 4 min • By Stupify Editorial

Marvel's First Family is making their grand entry into the MCU, set against a vibrant 1960s retro-futuristic backdrop. Here is everything we know about their unique multiverse origin.

A World Unlike Mainstream Earth-616

Rather than forcing them directly into modern-day New York, First Steps establishes a distinct, technologically advanced 1960s universe. Flying cars, sleek atomic design, and futuristic skylines set the aesthetic tone.

Enter Galactus and the Silver Surfer

With planetary devastation threatening their world, Reed Richards, Sue Storm, Johnny Storm, and Ben Grimm face the cosmic entity Galactus. The conflict forces the team to look beyond their universe for survival.

Bridging to Avengers: Doomsday

Rumors suggest that an imminent incursion will destroy their home universe, forcing Marvel's First Family to flee directly into Earth-616 just in time for the upcoming Avengers team-up.
