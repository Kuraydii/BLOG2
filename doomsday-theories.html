Is Doctor Doom Manipulating the Multiverse? Breaking Down the Avengers: Doomsday Theories

Published: Today • Reading Time: 5 min • By Stupify Editorial

As Phase 6 approaches its climax, rumors surround Victor Von Doom's true role in the impending multival collapse. Is he merely surviving the incursions, or actively directing the collision of realities?

The Mechanics of Incursions

First introduced in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, incursions happen when two realities collide, destroying one or both universes entirely. While the TVA attempts to maintain stability, Victor Von Doom operates in the shadows, drawing power directly from the collapse.

"I do not follow the timeline. I dictate its survival." — Victor Von Doom (Secret Wars #1)

The Doom Variant Question

Speculation runs rampant on whether this version of Doom is a variant of an established MCU hero or an ancient, multidimensional conqueror who pre-dates the Sacred Timeline. His technological mastery combined with mystic arts makes him uniquely equipped to pull the strings behind the scenes.

What This Means for Secret Wars

If Doom is orchestrating these events, Battleworld won't just be a battlefield—it will be his custom-built domain. Expect high-stakes team-ups, unexpected betrayals, and a total reconfiguration of the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
