'X-Men '97' Season 2 Teasers: Magneto's Fate and the Age of Apocalypse Setup

Published: 1 Week Ago • Reading Time: 5 min • By Stupify Editorial

After a jaw-dropping Season 1 finale that scattered our mutant heroes across time, Season 2 promises to dive deep into comic lore with Apocalypse at the center.

Lost in Time: Ancient Egypt and the Far Future

Rogue, Beast, Nightcrawler, Charles Xavier, and Magneto find themselves in 3000 BC Ancient Egypt, meeting a young En Sabah Nur. Meanwhile, Cyclops and Jean Grey land in 3960 AD, crossing paths with Clan Askani and a young Nathan Summers.

Magneto's Conflict and Redemption

Magneto's trial and subsequent choices in Season 1 tested the line between hero and villain. In Season 2, facing the rise of Apocalypse in ancient history will test his ideology like never before.

Adapting 'Age of Apocalypse'

With Apocalypse holding a Gambit card in modern-day Genosha during the finale, fans can expect the introduction of the Four Horsemen—with heartbreaking returns and high-stakes battles.
