Spider-Man 4 Speculation: Street-Level Hero or Multiversal Renegade?

Published: 2 Weeks Ago • Reading Time: 4 min • By Stupify Editorial

Following Doctor Strange's memory-wiping spell at the end of No Way Home, Peter Parker is completely isolated. Where does the web-slinger go from here?

Back to Basics: Grounded Street-Level Vigilante

Without Stark tech, Avengers support, or friends, Peter is back in a classic cloth suit listening to police scanners. Fans are eager to see him team up with Daredevil to battle Kingpin in the gritty alleys of New York City.

The Multiversal Pull

However, with Avengers: Doomsday on the horizon, can Spider-Man stay grounded for long? Sony and Marvel Studios must balance intimate character-driven storytelling with the overarching Multiverse Saga.

Potential Allies and Antagonists

From the potential arrival of Felicia Hardy (Black Cat) to the lingering symbiote piece left behind in Mexico, Peter Parker's next chapter is ripe with narrative possibilities.
